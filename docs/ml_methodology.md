The demo `placed` label is generated using a seeded probabilistic rule correlated with CGPA, internship/project experience, aptitude, communication and backlogs. IDs are excluded. Logistic Regression and Random Forest share a stratified holdout and train-only preprocessing; cross-validation F1 on training selects the model. Metrics are synthetic-demo only and do not establish real-world performance. Feature influence is not causation. No calibrated confidence or fairness claim is made.
